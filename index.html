Physics Practicals — Experiments & Activities Manual

Experiments (1–8)

1. Ohm's Law & Screw Gauge: R = V/I from V–I graph slope; LC = 0.01 mm; ρ = R·π(d/2)² / L.

2. Metre Bridge - 1: X = R(100 − l)/l.

3. Convex Lens: f = uv/(u + v); plot u–v and 1/u–1/v graphs with same scale & origin.

4. Convex Mirror: Normal ray retracing with auxiliary convex lens gives f = R/2.

5. Refractive Index of Liquid: μ = 2 − fg/f.

6. Figure of Merit: G = RS/(R − S) and k = E / [(R + G)θ].

7. Concave Lens: f = uv/(u − v) (lenses out of contact).

8. Metre Bridge - 2: Verify series combination R3 = R1 + R2.

Activities (1–6)

Act 1: Identify Diode, LED, Resistor, Capacitor, Transistor, IC. Act 2: Carbon resistor colour code vs multimeter. Act 3: Open circuit diagram rules. Act 4: Convex lens image positions. Act 5: Assembling Ohm's law circuit. Act 6: Glass slab lateral displacement d = t sec r sin(i − r).
