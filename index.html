📚 Physics Practical Lab Manual

      

        
EXP 1Ohm's Law & Screw Gauge
R = V/I, LC = 0.01 mm, ρ = R·A / L

        
EXP 2Metre Bridge - 1
X = R(100 − l)/l (30 cm ≤ l ≤ 70 cm)

        
EXP 3Convex Lens
f = uv/(u + v); plot u–v & 1/u–1/v graphs

        
EXP 4Convex Mirror
f = R/2 using auxiliary convex lens L

        
EXP 5Refractive Index of Liquid
μ = 2 − fg/f via needle parallax

        
EXP 6Figure of Merit
G = RS/(R − S), k = E/((R + G)θ)

        
EXP 7Concave Lens
f = uv/(u − v) (lenses out of contact)

        
EXP 8Metre Bridge - 2
Verify series law R3 = R1 + R2
